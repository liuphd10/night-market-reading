# 夜市識字 · Night Market Reading

A small browser game for kids who speak Mandarin and are learning to read it, starting with Taiwanese street food (Traditional characters).

- **字卡 Flashcards**: 36 characters for food, taste and ordering, each with a picture, pinyin and a memory tip.
- **認字攤 Stall 1**: see a character, pick the picture (or the reverse).
- **拼菜名 Stall 2**: put characters in order to spell 18 real dishes.
- **點菜王 Stall 3**: order for five customers from a menu with no pictures, pick sugar and ice levels, and pay the right total.
- **拼音 switch**: shows pinyin above every character.

Play it at https://liuphd10.github.io/night-market-reading/ . Progress is saved in the browser.
